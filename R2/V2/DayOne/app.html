What is an ID card?

  • One card for one part of your lifeA trip, dating, free trials, shopping — give each its own card.
  • Put anything on itEmail, phone number, payment card or password. Your real ones, or aliases Cloaked makes for you.
  • Hand out the card, not yourselfCalls, emails and charges that come back show up under that card, so you always know who used it.
  • Your items stay safeEverything on a card is still in your Vault. Deleting a card doesn’t delete its items.
